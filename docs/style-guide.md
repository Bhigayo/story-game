# BRIT LIFE style guide (from the ChatGPT concept art)

Mood: Birmingham at golden hour into dusk. Warm amber light on brick, purple-indigo shadows and sky, glowing windows.

## World palette
- Sky: horizon amber #F59E73, rose #B8618F, zenith indigo #2B296B (SkyDome in Atmosphere.tsx)
- Fog: #C08A9E. Sun: #FFB070. Hemisphere: #9DA3E8 over #5B4660
- Windows: warm #FFB257 glow (bloom picks them up)
- Grade: bloom 0.55, saturation +0.18, contrast +0.08, vignette (PostFX.tsx)

## UI rules (next phase)
- Dark glass panels (rgba 14,12,28,0.72, blur), 14px radius, 1px light border
- Gold #F5C451 for level, money highlights and active states; green #6EE7A0 for progress; purple #A78BFA for places and markers
- Top bar: logo, LVL badge, XP bar, nav tabs. Left: objectives and city feed. Right: wallet and needs. Bottom right: round minimap and location pill.

1 Global look (done) 2 UI redesign 3 Clothed characters 4 World dressing (venue icons, billboards, landmarks) 5 Interiors
